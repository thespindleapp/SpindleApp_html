06 — Frequently asked

The questions real product teams ask first.

01
Does Spindle read my whole inbox?
No. When you install the plugin, you choose which senders or labels Spindle should watch — typically your Suppliers folder, a list of factory contacts, or everyone outside your own domain. Spindle reads only that scope. Personal mail, internal team threads, and the replies you send are never accessed or stored. You can change scope, pause, or revoke access at any time from the plugin settings.
02
Will it get the style number wrong?
Sometimes. About 8% of supplier emails are ambiguous about which style they relate to — usually because the email refers to "the khaki one" or has the style number only in an attached PDF. Spindle flags these as "needs your eyes" with a one-tap confirmation right in the sidebar. Every correction trains the model on your specific brand vocabulary.
03
Does it replace my PLM?
No. Spindle works alongside Centric, Bamboo Rose, Backbone, or your homegrown Excel — whichever you use. We capture the comms layer that PLMs were never designed for. Most customers keep their PLM for the formal record and use Spindle for the daily work.
04
What about WhatsApp? Half my comms are there.
In v1, you can forward a WhatsApp message (text or screenshot) into your own inbox and Spindle reads it the same way it reads everything else. Real WhatsApp Business API integration is on the roadmap for the second half of this year. We're not pretending it's seamless yet — but the forward workaround is fine for the first 100 brands.
05
What if our IT won't approve a plugin?
Then Spindle gives you a private inbox address and you forward supplier mail to it manually — same Spindle, same style cards, no install required. Works on Outlook 2016, Apple Mail, mobile, anything. About 1 in 5 brands we've spoken to start this way while their IT review runs in parallel; many stay on the forward path indefinitely.
