FAQ

Is PLM only for big brands?
The big suites were built for big brands, but there are lighter, cheaper tools aimed at small teams. See What is PLM for fashion?
Can't I just use a project management tool?
For tasks and deadlines, yes. For style-level details, less so. We cover that in Can you run fashion production in Asana, Trello or Notion?
What if I'm a one-person brand?
You can probably stay on a good spreadsheet for now. Revisit when you hire, add a season, or catch yourself retyping emails every evening.
