Write it down the moment it's decided.

Most production problems start when a decision is made on a call or in a thread and never written down. This pack is about writing it down, quickly, at the moment it happens. It goes with our guide, The fashion production process, stage by stage.

p. 16

When email gets too much

This pack works with email and Excel, and it will take you a long way. The weak point is the saving and the checking. Someone has to remember to file each confirmation and link it to the right style.

That is the part Spindle is built for. You forward supplier emails to a Spindle address. It links each one to the right style and proposes the stage, quantity or date it finds. You check each one, and a production board is built from the values you have checked. Spindle is not a full PLM. It covers the email gap between the stages.
